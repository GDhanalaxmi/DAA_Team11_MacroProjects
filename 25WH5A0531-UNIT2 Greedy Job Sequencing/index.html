ALGORITHM VISUALIZER

Greedy Job Sequencing with Deadlines

Watch how a greedy strategy chooses profitable jobs and fits them into time slots before their deadlines.

THE IDEA

Make each slot count

Each job takes one time unit. We consider jobs from highest profit to lowest, then place each one in the latest free slot on or before its deadline. If no slot fits, we skip it.

Why is it called Greedy?

It always considers the highest-profit job first and tries to place it in the latest available slot before its deadline.
